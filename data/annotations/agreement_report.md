# Inter-annotator agreement report (Phase 2)

Generated by `src/agreement.py`. Protocol: `data/annotations/protocol.md`.

> **Annotator A is Claude Code; annotator B is a human author (`DEVIATIONS.md` D-001).** The kappa below is therefore a **human-vs-AI** agreement statistic, not an inter-human one, and must be described as such in the paper.

## Adjudication policy

Every A/B disagreement was resolved in favour of **annotator B, the human author**, by a policy the authors fixed before this stage was run. `scripts_final.csv` therefore reproduces annotator B's tags exactly wherever annotator B gave one. The paper must describe adjudication as *human annotator authoritative*, not as case-by-case joint resolution.
